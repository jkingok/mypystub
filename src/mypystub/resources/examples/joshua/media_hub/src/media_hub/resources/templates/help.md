# Media Hub

This app maintains a shareable catalogue of movies and TV shows.

## How to use

Tap _Settings_ to set up the app. You will need an _API Key_ from [themoviedb](https://themoviedb.org) in order to download data.

You can also set the _Title_ of your catalogue here.

Tap _Add_ to find titles to add to your catalogue. You must search and navigate [themoviedb](https://themoviedb.org) until you get to the item page and then click _Add This_. Alternatively you can click _Manual Add_ and directly enter an item that does not exist online.

Tap _List_ to show your list. This view allows you to _Delete_ items by swiping, or tap into the item to update the data. You can use the _Subtitle_ as an extra comment.

Tap _View_ to show the formatted catalog. Click _Reload_ to regenerate it (this may be slow, so it's not automatic), and click _Share_ to distribute the database to others in "party mode".
